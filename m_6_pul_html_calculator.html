M6 PUL Calculator

Pregnancy of unknown location risk calculator with configurable coefficients.

This page implements the multinomial logistic structure of the M6 calculator. The workflow facts used here are supported by the accessible literature: the updated M6 model uses 0-hour β-hCG, 48-hour β-hCG ratio, and optionally initial progesterone; progesterone ≤ 2 nmol/L is the first-step low-risk rule in the two-step protocol; and a 5% EP/PPUL threshold is commonly used to define high risk. The exact published coefficient tables were not fully accessible in the sources I could inspect, so the coefficients are editable. Replace them with your verified coefficient set before clinical use.

Model equations used in this page

hCG ratio = hCG at 48 h / initial hCG
x1 = ln(hCG0)
x2 = ln(hCG ratio)
x3 = ln(progesterone)
x2² = [ln(hCG ratio)]²

LP(EP) = intercept + b1·x1 + b2·x2 + b3·x2² + b4·x3
LP(IUP) = intercept + c1·x1 + c2·x2 + c3·x2² + c4·x3

P(EP) = exp(LP(EP)) / [1 + exp(LP(EP)) + exp(LP(IUP))]
P(IUP) = exp(LP(IUP)) / [1 + exp(LP(EP)) + exp(LP(IUP))]
P(FPUL) = 1 / [1 + exp(LP(EP)) + exp(LP(IUP))]

Evidence notes

The 2024 updated M6 paper confirms that the updated M6P and M6NP models use the same predictor variables as the original versions, specifically log(β-hCG at 0 h) and log(β-hCG ratio), with M6P also incorporating progesterone. It also describes the two-step protocol where progesterone ≤ 2 nmol/L can classify a case as low risk without waiting for the second hCG, and it reports the 5% EP/PPUL threshold used clinically. The accessible thesis snippet also indicates that M6NP includes a squared log(hCG ratio) term. Exact coefficient tables were not available in the accessible text I could review, so the page keeps coefficients editable for verification against your source document.
